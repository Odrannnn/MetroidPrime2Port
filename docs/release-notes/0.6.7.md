This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Smoother frame rate:** fewer empty GPU passes, and the scan peek is cheaper. #33
- **Lens flares are hidden behind objects again** with anti-aliasing on. #35
- **Title screen music keeps looping** instead of cutting out after about 85 seconds. #37
- **Looping music and sounds no longer cut out before restarting**, as in Dark Agon Temple. #38
- **Rapid-fire cannon sounds no longer drop out.** #10
- **The logbook scroll prompt shows the button of the device you are using.** #34
- **Trilogy discs show the right buttons** for the logbook, the map key, the world toggle and item zoom. #39
- **Morph Ball effects are back:** ghost trail, boost swooshes, spider glass, wall sparks, dark visor and damage flashes.
- **Rain splashes on Samus and creatures**, and snow drifts with the wind as in the original.
- **Scan Visor colours are correct with anti-aliasing on.**
- **Credits are centred in widescreen.**
- **The F1 Cheats key menu lists all nine Sky Temple Keys.**
- **Enemy and boss behaviour matches the original more closely** (Grenchler, Dark Trooper, Lumite, Chykka and others).
