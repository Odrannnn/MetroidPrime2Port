This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Map tracker:** pickup markers on the map (F1 > Tracker: off, by room or every pickup), an optional Reveal map, and the rooms you have visited in each world, with a list of the ones you haven't.
- **Morph Ball camera control** (Controls > Options, off by default): the mouse, the right stick, touch swipes or the C-stick move the camera around the ball. #54
- **Light world music in Dark Aether** (F1 > Enhancements > Music, off by default): each dark region plays its light world theme. Boss and event music do not change. #36
- **Crosshair opacity goes up to 500%**, so you can get back the brighter crosshair from before 0.7.0. #56
- **Flick stick** (Controls > Stick aim, twin stick only): tilt the right stick to turn and face that way, then rotate it to keep turning. Touch controls have their own Flick stick switch.
- **A controller preset sticks to its pad slot:** a controller you connect later gets the preset that slot last used.
- **Mouse aim no longer takes over a controller you are holding:** the pad keeps its own controls.
- **Touch: the Visor and Beam wheels are now buttons you add** to the touch layout, no longer an F1 checkbox.
- **The Battleground fight no longer ends after four Ing kills.** #46
- **Chykka's spit attacks work again.** #59
- **The Dark Visor's red highlights stay inside the lens** in widescreen and at any HUD scale. #60
- **The ball HUD's boss energy bar stays inside its frame** in widescreen. #50
- **Android: the left stick moves you forward again with Twin Stick Aim off.** It no longer strafes, and LB is no longer taken by the touch layout's beam switching. #58
- **Save states:** loading one on a moving elevator no longer leaves you stuck (Dark Torvus Temple). #61 Loading one in Mine Shaft no longer brings back enemies the room didn't have. #52
- **Less water flicker and fewer missing draws on a first run:** the shader cache covers both MSAA settings and more of the game. #44
- **Accuracy fixes found by checking against the original game:** Elite Pirate screen shakes, moving platforms that turn to face their target, enemies' Echo Visor sound sources, and the draw order of the Splitter's head, Ing blobs and Chykka's splashes.
