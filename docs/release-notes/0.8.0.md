This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Multiplayer works:** 2 to 4 player split screen, Deathmatch and Bounty, through to the results screen. #45
- **Each multiplayer player has their own controls:** the new F1 > Multiplayer page picks a player's controller and gives them their own Remap, presets, twin stick, stick speeds, aim assist and gyro. Players 2-4 keep their bindings in `controls_p2.toml` to `controls_p4.toml`. #45
- **The Remastered controller preset matches Metroid Prime Remastered's Dual Sticks:** map on View/Back, pause on Menu/Start, fire also on B, jump also on LB, beams on Y + D-pad. #45
- **Button prompts show the face button** when an action is bound to two buttons (Remastered fire shows B, not RT).
- **New Dodge binding:** a sideways dash while strafing, with or without lock-on.
- **New options:** Toggle Spider Ball, Aim Lock-On (targets the enemy nearest the crosshair), hand on cannon when standing still, Morph Ball black bars, and the crosshair in the Dark and Echo Visors.
- **Fewer stutters on a first run:** the game's shaders compile in the background from the start, on desktop and Android.
- **Save states keep the room's layers**, so a loaded save in the Battleground no longer replays its key event. #46 Save states from 0.7.0 no longer load.
- **Menus keep their titles and Confirm/Back prompts** in 16:10 and 16:9.
- **The main menu's selection orb shows keyboard keys** instead of a blank glow.
- **The touch back arrow leaves the pause menu** with the Remastered preset.
