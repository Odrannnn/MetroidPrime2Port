This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Infinite ammo lets you fire at zero**, and the HUD shows ∞ for each counter you pick (F1 > Cheats). #27
- **Combo cost and beam ammo per expansion sliders** in F1 > Cheats, each with a reset button. Values above 250 are kept in a separate file next to the memory card, so your save stays as it is. #27
- **No Dark Aether damage cheat**: Dark Aether's air does not hurt, as with the Light Suit; light crystals still heal. #27
- **Rotating rooms: Original/Fast** for the turning-room cutscene in the energy controller rooms. #27
- **Stack beams can leave out the Dark Beam.** #27
- **Echoes menu theme** for the F1 menu: black panels, white borders, violet accent. #27
- **Android: the touch F1 button** opens the menu reliably. #27
- **No more crash after the Dark Alpha Splinter's death cutscene.** #29
- **Morph Ball HUD threat gauge** lines up in widescreen. #25
- **Multiplayer:** Hacker Mode no longer takes the campaign's Scan Visor, and the world picker names the multiplayer arenas. #30
- **Unlock beam doors cheat works on Echoes doors**, now in F1 > Debug > Cheats.
- **Texture packs:** turn your pack on and off with F7, and import a pack from a `.zip` on desktop.
- **Saved binding presets** under Remap > My presets.
- **Photo mode:** Samus holds her arm cannon, and on touch screens the left-side movement stick is drawn.
- **Trilogy helmet:** the helmet's battery lights are coloured instead of white.
- **Changing the controls profile no longer counts a held key as a new press.**
