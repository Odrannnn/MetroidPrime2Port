This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Scannable objects no longer get stuck pulsing red or yellow and unscannable** until the room reloads. #51
- **Fixes random crashes in 0.8.0** (going through portals, picking up keys, morphing): the same scan visor bug wrote past the end of its memory in larger rooms. #53
- **Water no longer flickers or loses its refraction** when something splashes into it. #44
