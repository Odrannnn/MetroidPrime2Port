This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Third-person camera** (F1 > Video): Samus's body animates, jumps, flips, dashes and grapples, and the camera hands over to the Morph Ball camera and back.
- **GameCube saves carry over to the Trilogy disc,** USA and PAL: the Memory card page imports them (F1 > System > Memory card > Restore), and a first Trilogy start offers it. Import and Restore are no longer greyed out on the title and file select screens. #76
- **The Trilogy disc opens on the GameCube title screen** with its title music.
- **Ending a Trilogy multiplayer match no longer closes the game.** #76
- **Android: Replace disc image works** when the data folder is in app storage. F1 > System > Disc image names the running disc's version. #76
- **Exit Game on the main menu:** hold Z, or a finger or mouse button on it, for 1.5 s. #80
- **Water in Agon's lagoon no longer flickers.** #78
- **A save state made during a cutscene resumes it,** and save states keep every scripted object's state. #84
- **Cleaner cutscene skips:** no cinematic frames after the skip, aim waits until control comes back, and a dialog's A press turns the page. Save-state loads and quick resume stay black until Samus is back. Thanks to rivyr. #79 #83
- **Fewer music crashes:** the game heap is locked while a disc read fills the music buffer. Thanks to rivyr. #75
- **Difficulty sliders** (F1 > Game > Difficulty): damage taken and damage dealt, x0.1 to x10, single player.
- **Shoot in Scan Visor** (F1 > Controls > Buttons): the arm cannon stays out and fires while scanning.
- **New cheats** (F1 > Cheats): moon jump, Boost Ball speed and duration, boost anywhere, steer while boosting. The free camera can put Samus where it is (F1 > Debug > Camera > Move Samus here).
- **Texture packs free memory when RAM or video memory runs low,** and the `user_textures` folder is created at startup.
- **Fewer stutters on first play:** the shader cache covers more of the game, and fog no longer needs its own shaders. #78
- **The lore scan's Start prompt shows the bound button.** Thanks to rivyr. #82
