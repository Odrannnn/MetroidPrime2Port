This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Seamless item pickups** (F1 > Enhancements > Pace, off by default): a pickup no longer stops the game. Its "acquired" message shows as a HUD memo and the acquisition cutscene is skipped. #66
- **Sprint:** a Custom walk speed switch (Enhancements > Pace) and a new While held mode for custom actions, so a held button can sprint at the Walk speed slider's speed. #66
- **Map controls on the remap page:** Map rotate and Map pan rows act only on the map screen, so keys like WASD can pan the map without changing your gameplay bindings. The map's button prompts follow them. #31
- **Click or tap a room on the area map to select it** (with Controls > Options > Point and click menus on). #66
- **The crosshair shows while you aim freely under a lock-on**, so you can see where your shots go. #66
- **Android: custom actions appear in the touch layout editor.** #66
- **Touch controls come back on the map and logbook** after a key or controller hid them.
- **Quick resume on a card that was never saved** no longer plays the opening mission text first.
- **Sanctuary Entrance's and Central Mining Station's door cutscenes no longer replay** after loading a save state. #67
- **The multiplayer results screen has its proper colours** instead of a red tint. #64
- **The Echo Visor no longer fills the screen with orange and white bands** near Quadraxis's antennas. #65
- **Trilogy controls: the next-target arrows no longer jitter,** and the GameCube-style crosshair follows Crosshair size again.
- **Faster startup:** the shader cache no longer checks every page on start.
- **Fixed memory corruption in particle effects** whose particle count grows after they start.
- **HUD fixes:** the damage ring and lock-on indicator draw as in the original game, the Morph Ball threat bar's fill stays inside its frame, and the camera filter dialog box keeps its shape in widescreen.
- **Fewer enemies and effects culled at the screen edges** with a wider field of view, photo mode or the free camera.
